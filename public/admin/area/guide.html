Upcoming meetings: how to use this editor

This editor changes the Upcoming meetings on the Events page, the Area Service page and the home page: the monthly Area Service meeting and one-off meetings like JAC or budget meetings. It cannot change anything else on the site.

Logging in

Bookmark this editor: /admin/area/. Log in with the committee email and password you set from the invite email. The committee login is shared by whoever holds the position, so nobody's own name ever shows on the site or in its history.

Area Service every month

You do not enter Area Service each month. The site works out the next date by itself from the rule at the top: the 3rd Tuesday, 7:30 PM, Westwood United Methodist Church. Only change the rule if the regular day, time or place changes for good. Times there are 24-hour: 19:30 is 7:30 PM.

Cancel or move one month

  1. Under Cancel or move one month, press Add change.
  2. Pick the date the meeting would normally fall on.
  3. Choose Cancelled or Moved. For moved, pick the new date and time.
  4. Add a short note if it helps, then press Publish.

Add a one-off meeting (JAC, budget, anything else)

  1. Under Other meetings, press Add meeting.
  2. Fill in the title and when it starts. Add the place, or the Zoom link and details for a virtual meeting.
  3. Press Publish.

Change or remove one

Open it, change what you need, and press Publish. To take one down early, press the small x on its row, then Publish.

Past meetings

Meetings drop off the site by themselves once they end. You do not need to delete them, and old cancel or move changes can stay.

Dates and times

Pick the date and time in the box. Times are New Jersey time. The site stores them like 2027-06-12T12:00, which means June 12, 2027 at 12:00 noon. You never need to type a time zone.

Saving

Press Publish at the top. The live site updates by itself in a minute or two. If you do not see the change, wait a moment and reload the page.
